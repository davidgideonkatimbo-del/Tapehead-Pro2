# Tapehead Pro v1.10.36 — Studio Color Refinement

## Goal
Reduce bright orange accents and establish a restrained studio palette.

## Palette
- Black / deep charcoal for the studio base.
- Water blue-teal for interactive/active sequencing states.
- Muted gold for primary highlights, active navigation, playback and important actions.
- Status colors such as red remain reserved for warnings/recording states.

## Notes
- No Beat Maker features were removed.
- AI and interactive controls were shifted from bright purple to water tones.
- Orange primary gradients were replaced with muted gold.
- Service-worker cache bumped to v11036.
