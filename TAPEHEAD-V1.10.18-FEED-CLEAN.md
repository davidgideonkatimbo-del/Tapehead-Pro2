# Tapehead Pro v1.10.18 — Feed Clean Social Upgrade

The Feed has been redesigned around listening and discovery rather than exposing every control at once.

## Main views
- For You — newest posts
- Popular — engagement-ranked posts
- Following — posts from followed artists
- Collab — posts marked open to collaboration

## Secondary controls
Search and advanced genre/media filters remain available without dominating the main Feed.

## Post actions
Like, comment, remix, and Add Vocal are retained. Share is available through existing project/share flows rather than occupying every post's primary action row.

## Mobile
The main tabs scroll horizontally, the search field and controls remain compact, and cards use tighter spacing while retaining large touch targets.
