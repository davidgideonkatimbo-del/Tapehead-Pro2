# Tapehead Pro — User Manual

**Version:** 1.8.0  
**For:** Tapehead Pro web, Android and iOS builds

## Welcome to Tapehead Pro

Tapehead Pro is a mobile-first music creation studio for capturing ideas, writing lyrics, building beats, shaping chords, recording vocals, mixing songs, and sharing work with other artists.

The basic workflow is simple:

**Idea → Write → Keys → Beat → Record → Mix → Bounce → Share**

You do not have to complete every stage. Save a project whenever you have an idea and return to it later.

---

## 1. Getting started

### Open Tapehead

From the main screen you can access:

- **New Project** — start a fresh song.
- **My Profile** — view and edit your artist profile.
- **Write Lyrics** — write and organize lyrics.
- **Keys Chords** — create or explore chord ideas.
- **Beat Sequencer** — program a drum pattern.
- **Record Vocals** — record a take against your project.
- **Mix Bounce** — balance your tracks and prepare a full-song bounce.
- **Feed Share** — publish and discover music.
- **Collab Rooms** — work with other artists.
- **Messages** — manage private conversations.
- **Help & Manual** — open this guide.

The **☰ menu** provides access to the main areas of the app, account controls, theme settings and help.

### Create an account

Choose **Create Account** and enter your artist information. Create an account with your email or phone and artist name. Cloud accounts sync Feed, messages, and collab across devices.

New accounts may be offered a **30-day Pro trial**. Trial and Pro availability are controlled by the current account and server configuration.

If you already have an account, choose **Sign In**.

> **Tip:** Use an account if you want your cloud-based social, collaboration and cross-device features to follow you between devices.

# 2. Your first song

A simple first session can be completed in a few minutes:

1. Select **New Project**.
2. Give the song a working title.
3. Open **Write Lyrics** and create a Verse and Chorus.
4. Open **Keys Chords** and choose or create a chord progression.
5. Open **Beat Sequencer** and make a simple 8-, 16- or 32-step pattern.
6. Open **Record Vocals** and record a take.
7. Open **Mix Bounce** and balance the song.
8. Choose **Bounce Full Song** when you want a WAV export.
9. Choose **Publish Session** when you want to share the work to Feed.

You can return to any stage and make changes without rebuilding the whole project.

# 3. Write Lyrics

The **Write** workspace is your lyric desk.

### Organize a song

Use section tabs such as:

- Intro
- Verse
- Pre
- Chorus
- Bridge
- Outro

Select **＋ Section** to add another section when your arrangement needs one.

### Write manually

Enter your lyrics directly into the section editor. Keep your first version simple; you can polish it later.

The workspace also provides syllable information to help you judge how comfortably a line can fit the musical idea.

### AI songwriting tools

When Cloud AI is available, Tapehead provides:

- **Finish Verse** — continue a verse from your starting material.
- **Write Next** — develop the next part of the song.
- **Polish** — refine existing writing while keeping the idea intact.
- **Hook Lab** — develop hook ideas.
- **Create Full Song** — expand a concept into a larger song structure.

AI works best when you provide a clear idea, emotion, story, subject or starting lines. You can also control available style, language and length options.

If Cloud AI is unavailable, the app can continue to provide a local songwriting fallback for supported actions.

### Share or export writing

Use the available **Share**, **Copy / Export**, and collaboration actions to move your lyrics into the next stage or share them with others.

# 4. Keys & Chords

The **Keys** workspace helps you develop the harmonic foundation of a song.

### Chord progressions

Use the four progression slots to build a sequence. Available chord choices include common major and minor chords and other displayed options.

You can:

- Play chord pads.
- Change octave.
- Select a scale mode when available.
- Play or preview the progression.
- Copy the chord ideas for use elsewhere.

### Hum → Chord

Use **Record Hum** to capture a short melodic idea. Tapehead can analyze the idea and provide chord suggestions where supported.

Use **Apply to Beat + Write** when you want the generated musical idea to become part of the current project.

### Tempo

The project BPM acts as the common musical clock across the relevant Beat, Keys and Record workspaces. Adjust it to match the feel you want.

---

# 5. Beat Sequencer

The **Beat** workspace is a step sequencer for building drum patterns.

### Choose the pattern length

Choose:

- **8 steps** — quick ideas and simple loops.
- **16 steps** — the standard starting point for a two-bar style pattern.
- **32 steps** — longer and more detailed patterns.

### Program drums

Tap steps for the available drum voices, such as:

- Kick
- Snare
- Hi-Hat
- Clap

Press **Play** or **Hear Beat** to audition the pattern.

### Speed up beat creation

- **Randomize** — generate a new rhythmic idea.
- **Duplicate** — copy the current pattern.
- **Clear** — remove the current pattern after confirmation.
- **Edit on Beat** — return to the sequencer when another workspace exposes the beat.

Genre/kit choices can change the character of the drum pattern.

> **Tip:** Start with a kick and snare pattern before adding extra percussion. A simple groove is easier to build on.

# 6. Record Vocals

The **Record** workspace lets you capture a vocal performance against the project.

### Before recording

For the cleanest take:

- Use headphones when possible.
- Record in a quiet room.
- Keep the microphone a consistent distance from your mouth.
- Check your input level before committing to a full take.

### Record a take

1. Select the appropriate section on the timeline.
2. Preview the beat and musical backing.
3. Press the red **REC** control.
4. Perform your vocal.
5. Stop the recording when finished.
6. Use **Play Take** to review it.

The recording workflow can use the project beat, chords and click where available.

### Fix timing

If a take feels early or late, use:

- **Nudge ‹** — move the take earlier.
- **Nudge ›** — move the take later.

Use small adjustments. The goal is to improve alignment without changing the character of the performance.

### Send to Mix

Choose **Send to Mix** when the take is ready to be balanced against the rest of the song.

---

# 7. Mix & Bounce

The **Mix** workspace is where the separate elements become a song.

### Balance the tracks

Use the available controls for:

- Kick
- Snare
- Hi-Hat
- Clap
- Chords
- Vocal

You can use **Mute** and **Solo** to isolate parts while listening.

### Quick mix presets

Where available, use presets such as:

- Balanced
- Vocal Up
- Club
- Radio
- Soft Demo

These are starting points. Adjust the individual tracks after choosing a preset.

### A/B checking

Use **A/B** when available to compare mix approaches without losing the original idea.

### Mix checklist

Before bouncing, check:

- The song has a clear title.
- The main lyric sections are present.
- The beat is playing correctly.
- The vocal is audible.
- No track is unintentionally muted.
- Levels are not obviously clipping or excessively loud.
- The song plays from beginning to end as expected.

### Bounce Full Song

Choose **🎧 Bounce Full Song** to create the available full-song WAV output.

The resulting file can then be saved, shared or moved to another workflow supported by your device.

### Save Project

Use **Save Project** regularly, especially after major changes.

# 8. Feed & Sharing

The **Feed** is Tapehead's social space for discovering and sharing work.

### Publish

From a project, choose **Publish Session** or **Post** and provide the requested information about the session.

You can share a work-in-progress or a finished idea. If you want collaborators, mark the post as open to collaboration where the option is available.

### Discover music

Use Feed filters such as:

- Latest
- Popular
- My Posts
- Open to Collab
- Genre
- Has Sound
- Has Vocal

Use the search field to find songs or artists.

### Post actions

Depending on the post, you can:

- Play available audio.
- Open the project.
- Like a post.
- Comment.
- Follow an artist.
- Message an artist.
- Report or block an account when necessary.

> **Remember:** Only publish material you have the right to share.

# 9. Collaboration Rooms

**Collab** lets artists work together around a shared project.

### Create a room

1. Open **Collab Rooms**.
2. Choose **Create Room** from the current project.
3. Share the generated room code or invite information.
4. Wait for collaborators to join.

### Join a room

Choose **Join**, enter or paste the room code, and connect to the room.

### Collaboration controls

Depending on the room and permissions, participants can use features such as:

- **Pull Beat** — bring the room's beat into the working session.
- **Push Beat** — share the current beat with the room.
- **Claim Empty** — claim an available contribution area.
- **Merge Sections → Write** — bring collaborative material into the writing workspace.
- **Mark Ready** — indicate that your part is ready.
- Room notes and chat.

Use the room chat to communicate changes before making major edits.

### Leaving a room

Use **Leave** when you are finished. Room permissions may restrict certain actions for the host.

# 13. Theme and mobile use

Use **Theme** to switch the available appearance mode.

Tapehead is designed for touch screens. On Android and iOS:

- Tap controls directly rather than relying on hover.
- Rotate the device only when the current screen supports the orientation.
- Allow microphone access when prompted if you want to record or use the hum/chord feature.
- Use headphones when recording to reduce microphone bleed.

If a control appears unresponsive, first close an open dialog or overlay and try again. If necessary, reload the app so the latest cached application shell is restored.

# 14. Saving, offline use and cloud data

Tapehead has local application behavior for core project work and can also use cloud services for accounts and social features.

Cloud-backed features can include:

- Accounts and profiles
- Feed posts
- Likes and comments
- Following
- Private messages
- Collaboration rooms and chat
- Pro/account services
- Cloud AI, where configured

Availability depends on your connection and the server configuration.

### Offline use

The installed web application includes an offline-capable service-worker shell. This allows the application interface to load from its cached resources when network access is unavailable.

Cloud actions naturally require connectivity and may wait until you are back online.

> **Important:** Offline availability is not the same as a complete offline copy of every cloud feature or remote account record.

# 15. Troubleshooting

## A button does not respond

1. Close any open modal or dialog.
2. Tap the button again.
3. If the app has just been updated, reload it so the newest application files are used.
4. If installed as a PWA/app, fully close and reopen Tapehead.

## The profile does not open

Reload Tapehead and try **My Profile** again. The current build uses a dedicated touch-friendly profile control and a fallback event path for mobile environments.

## AI does not respond

Check your internet connection and try again. Cloud AI requires the server-side AI route and its configured environment variables. If the cloud service is unavailable, supported local songwriting actions can still remain usable.

## Recording does not work

Check that the browser or mobile app has microphone permission. Close other applications that may be using the microphone, then reopen Tapehead.

## Audio playback is silent

Check device volume, mute state and the individual track controls. Make sure the track is not muted and that the project actually contains audio or a programmed beat.

## A cloud feature is unavailable

Confirm that you are signed in and connected to the internet. Some social, collaboration, account and Pro functions depend on the configured cloud services.

## A change does not appear after an update

The service worker may still be serving an older cached resource. Reload the app completely. If the problem persists, clear the site's cached data for Tapehead and reopen it.

# 16. Good recording and production habits

- Start with a short idea instead of trying to finish a perfect song immediately.
- Save after major changes.
- Keep a clear project title.
- Use headphones while recording.
- Leave enough headroom in the mix.
- Test the complete song before bouncing.
- Keep original ideas before making major edits.
- Share early drafts when you want collaborators, but only share material you are authorized to publish.

# 17. Privacy and responsible use

Tapehead may process account, profile, social, audio and project information depending on which features you use and which cloud services are enabled.

Review the app's **Privacy Policy** and **Terms** before using cloud and social features. Do not publish private information, copyrighted material you do not have permission to distribute, or another person's recording without authorization.

For production deployments, server secrets such as AI and service-role credentials must remain server-side and must never be placed in browser-exposed files.

## Final workflow

When you are ready to turn an idea into a finished song, use this sequence:

**1. Capture the idea**  
Write a line, hum a melody or start a beat.

**2. Build the foundation**  
Create a chord progression and drum pattern.

**3. Write the song**  
Develop the Verse, Chorus and other sections.

**4. Record**  
Capture your vocal and adjust timing if necessary.

**5. Mix**  
Balance the instruments and vocal.

**6. Bounce**  
Create the full-song WAV when the mix is ready.

**7. Share or collaborate**  
Publish to Feed or create a Collab Room.

**8. Keep creating**  
Save the project and come back whenever the next idea arrives.
