Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026 · Tapehead Pro · Operated by David Gideon Katimbo (Deon)

Tapehead Pro (“Tapehead”, “we”, “us”) is a music creation and collaboration service. This Privacy Policy explains what information we process, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • Account data — email address or phone number, password (stored by our authentication provider), artist username, and profile details you choose to add (bio, location, genres, social links, avatar).
  • Creative content — lyrics, projects, chord and beat data, audio recordings, and other material you create or upload.
  • Social activity — posts you publish to the Feed, likes, comments, follows, private messages, and collaboration-room activity.
  • Usage and technical data — device and browser type, approximate network information, app version, crash or error logs, and interaction needed to operate security, billing, and AI features.
  • Payment metadata — plan selection, transaction references, and entitlement status. Card and mobile-money details are handled by our payment provider (Pesapal); Tapehead does not store full payment card numbers.

2. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Create and secure your account, and keep you signed in across sessions and devices.
  • Save, sync, and restore your projects and studio settings.
  • Provide collaboration rooms, messaging, Feed, and public profile features you choose to use.
  • Process Pro trials, subscriptions, and lifetime purchases, and enforce paid feature access.
  • Provide AI songwriting features when you request them.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access, and improve reliability and performance.
  • Communicate service notices related to your account, security, or payments.

3. AI features

When you use Tapehead’s AI songwriting tools, the text you submit and relevant song context (such as section labels, style, language, and length preferences) are sent to our AI service provider so the requested generation can be completed. Do not submit confidential or sensitive information you do not want processed by that provider.

AI output may be inaccurate or unsuitable. You are responsible for reviewing generated material before publishing or commercially using it. We apply usage limits and account checks to reduce abuse of the AI service.

4. Audio and files

Recordings uploaded for collaboration are available to authorized room members under the product’s access controls. Signed access is used where required so private media is not left permanently public. You may delete recordings and request account deletion as described below.

5. Public content

Content you publish to the Feed, and public profile fields such as username, avatar, biography, genres, links, location, and collaboration availability, may be visible to other users according to the sharing options you use. Private messages and private project data are not intended for public display.

6. Sharing with service providers

We use trusted processors to operate the product, including:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, realtime delivery, and file storage.
  • OpenAI — AI text generation for requested songwriting actions.
  • Pesapal — payment checkout and transaction confirmation.
  • Hosting providers (such as Vercel) — delivery of the web application and serverless APIs.

These providers process data under their own security and contractual arrangements with us, solely to provide their services for Tapehead.

7. Security

We use authentication, access controls, encrypted transport (HTTPS), and server-side checks for sensitive operations such as AI usage, payments, and account deletion. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for activity under your account.

8. Retention and deletion

We retain account and project data while your account remains active and as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our terms.

You may request account deletion from your account settings in the app. Deletion removes your account and associated application data, subject to backup copies, legal retention requirements, and time needed to complete the deletion process. Content you published that other users still lawfully hold (for example comments on their posts) may remain until separately removed under our moderation or retention practices.

9. Children

Tapehead Pro is intended for users who can lawfully create an account and use online creative tools in their jurisdiction. If you believe a child has provided personal data without appropriate consent, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

10. International processing

Your information may be processed in countries where our providers operate. By using Tapehead you understand that data may be transferred to and stored in those locations under applicable safeguards used by our providers.

11. Your choices

  • Edit profile and public fields at any time in the app.
  • Choose what to publish to the Feed and what to keep private in projects or rooms.
  • Sign out, reset your password, or delete your account through the product flows provided.
  • Contact us to exercise applicable access, correction, or deletion rights under the law that applies to you.
